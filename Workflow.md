# Trade Finance Transaction Workflow

The following is a step-by-step workflow for a trade finance transaction on the platform:

1.  **Trade Agreement:** The exporter and importer agree on the terms of the trade, including the price, quantity, and shipping details.
2.  **LC Application:** The importer applies for a Letter of Credit (LC) from their bank.
3.  **LC Issuance:** The importer's bank issues the LC and sends it to the exporter's bank.
4.  **Shipment:** The exporter ships the goods to the importer.
5.  **Document Submission:** The exporter submits the shipping documents, including the invoice and bill of lading, to their bank.
6.  **Document Verification:** The exporter's bank verifies the documents and sends them to the importer's bank.
7.  **Payment:** The importer's bank makes the payment to the exporter's bank.
8.  **Goods Release:** The importer presents the shipping documents to the customs authority to get the goods released.
