# Deployment Plan

## 1. Proof-of-Concept (PoC)

The PoC will be deployed on a test network with a limited number of participants. The PoC will be used to validate the feasibility of the platform and to identify any potential issues.

## 2. Production Environment

Once the PoC has been successfully completed, the platform will be deployed to a production environment. The production environment will be a highly available and scalable platform that can support a large number of participants.

## 3. Roadmap

The following is a roadmap for scaling the platform to a production environment:

*   **Phase 1:** Onboard a small number of participants to the platform.
*   **Phase 2:** Add more participants to the platform and expand the scope of the platform to include other trade finance products.
*   **Phase 3:** Integrate the platform with other blockchain networks to create a global trade finance network.
